Design Desk mark — round 7

At rest the mark is DD: two D's facing right, each split in two. At work the first D turns round, the two close into a disc, the disc turns once, and it opens back into DD. The big animated mark beside each one shows it; so does "at work" in each row.

Round 7: three stages from quarters to spark, in the colours of Quarters, fuller. The spark twinkle is gone; at work the D's close into the disc and turn, as before. Shown at 16, 24, 32 and 48 px on the Desk's light and dark background, in a browser tab, as the agent's avatar in a thread, and on a GitHub comment line.
