# Grill me

The `grill` action ([docs/actions.md](../../../docs/actions.md)); on the Desk its steps and
records are in [docs/requests.md](../../../docs/requests.md) → Questions first.

## 1. Know what you can find yourself

Before asking, read the card, its research and the code of the screen. A question the code,
the issue or an earlier decision answers is never asked: use the answer and say where it came
from in the summary.

## 2. Walk the decision tree

List the decisions the design depends on, the ones that block others first: who it is for
and what they are trying to do, which screen and which moment, what must stay as it is, what
the request trades off (space, steps, what is shown first), and how the person pictures it.
Each answer can open or close branches; re-plan after each one.

## 3. One question at a time

- Ask exactly one question per turn, in the person's words, never in code's.
- Give 2 to 4 concrete answers and mark your suggested one; say in one line why it matters
  (`why`). "Leave it to Design Desk" is always possible.
- Never ask what the request already says, two things in one question, or a question whose
  answer would not change any option.
- At most 8 questions in all; most requests need 3 to 5.

## 4. Stop and sum up

Stop when every open decision is answered, left to you, or not worth a question. Write what
was agreed: one bullet per decision, with who decided (the person, or "left to Design Desk",
or the source you found). That summary is what the options follow.
